Squeeze your PDFs. Any size.

Free PDF compressor with no file-size limit and no upload. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

    How it works

    Each page is redrawn as an optimized image and rebuilt into a new PDF on your own device. That is why there is no size limit: the only limit is your device’s memory.

    Good to know

    Text in the compressed file becomes part of the page image, so it can’t be selected or searched. This works best for scans, slides and photo-heavy files. If a file can’t get any smaller, Squeeze keeps your original instead of making it bigger.
